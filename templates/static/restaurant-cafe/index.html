Our story

It started with one copper kettle and a borrowed oven

Meera and Arjun opened The Copper Kettle in 2016 with eight chairs, her grandmother's copper kettle and a secondhand oven that only worked if you jammed the door shut with a wooden spoon.

Ten years on, the oven has been replaced, but the idea hasn't: honest coffee from growers we know by name, bread made slowly by hand, and a room where neighbours become regulars and regulars become friends.

  • Small batch

    Roasted weekly in Chikmagalur

  • Slow dough

    48-hour fermented sourdough

  • Zero waste

    Day-old bakes go to local shelters
