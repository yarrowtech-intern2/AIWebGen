About

Hello, I’m Aarav.

I picked up my grandfather’s old film camera at fourteen and spent a summer photographing my family on the terrace of our home in Nashik. Those slightly blurry, sun-washed prints are still the most precious things I own — and they taught me that the best photographs aren’t perfect, they’re true.

Ten years and more than 180 weddings later, that’s still how I work. I stay close but unobtrusive, guiding gently when it helps and stepping back when it doesn’t. The result is a collection that feels like your day actually felt: the nervous laughter before the pheras, your nani’s tears, the friend who danced through every song.

Based in Mumbai, I photograph weddings across India and travel internationally a few times a year.
